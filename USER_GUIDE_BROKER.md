# Broker Field Guide: Sourcing Manager OS

Welcome to the Sourcing Manager OS. This app protects your leads and your commissions.

## 1. Why are phone numbers hidden?

Your lead data is your asset. The system uses a **Secure Call (PSTN Bridge)**.

- You click **"Secure Call"**.
- The system calls YOU first.
- Then it connects you to the client.
- Your personal number and the client's number are protected.

## 2. Site Visit = Commission Proof

To lock your commission for 45 days, a **Verified Site Visit** is mandatory.

- Use the app **AT THE SITE**.
- The app will verify your **GPS Location**.
- Take a **Live Photo** of the visitor or the site.
- Once verified, the system automatically creates a **Commission Lock**.

## 3. Tracking Your Earnings

Check the **Payout Ledger** to see:

- **Locked**: Visit verified, 45-day maturity pending.
- **Eligible**: Ready for payout.
- **Paid**: Payment completed.

## 4. Troubleshooting in the Field

- **"Outside Geofence"**: Site ke aur paas jayein. (Move closer to the site.)
- **"Network Weak"**: Signal area me jake retry karein.
- **"Loan Expired"**: Manager se access renew karne ko kahein.
